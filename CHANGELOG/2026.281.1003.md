## Summary

The OpenClaw family is consolidated into `opencharly/openclaw` (opencharly/opencharly#431), and its variant metalayers are **dropped rather than moved**: the family ships the gateway image and its layer, and no `-full`, `-desktop` or `-ml` variant returns.

- `charly.yml` — the `openclaw-full:` metalayer and its `openclaw-full-skill:` skill entity are deleted together. The manifest is now a comment-only tombstone, and the loader accepts it (measured below: 0 candies, 0 warnings, 0 entity keys).
- `README.md` / `AGENTS.md` — retired: they state what was dropped, what was **not** dropped, and where the family now lives, so no reader composes this repo or follows a skill for a layer that no longer exists.

**What is *not* retired — the part a reader could get wrong:** the tool layers this metalayer composed (codex, gemini, ripgrep, ffmpeg, gh, uv, tmux, sqlite, and the rest) belong to their own repos and stay available. What is gone is the pre-packaged "gateway plus everything" composition; a consumer that wants a tool composes it beside the gateway image directly. The tombstone says exactly that, in all three files.

**Named later steps in the same cutover (not deferred silently):** the umbrella submodule gitlink and the marketplace refs-list pin are removed in this cutover's later steps, and the operator archives the repo itself (out of scope here). `layer-openclaw-full-ml`, which composed this layer, is retired alongside it.

**Deliberate survivor:** `CHANGELOG/` keeps this repo's release history, which is where history belongs; the sweep below excludes it for exactly that reason.
